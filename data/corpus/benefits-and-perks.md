# Northwind Robotics — Benefits & Perks

## Health Insurance

Northwind covers 100% of the medical, dental, and vision premium for employees
and 75% of the premium for dependents. Coverage begins on the first day of the
month following your start date. There is no waiting period beyond that.

Employees may choose between two medical plans: a PPO plan and a high-deductible
health plan (HDHP). Employees who elect the HDHP receive an annual company
contribution of $1,200 into their Health Savings Account (HSA).

## Retirement (401k)

Northwind matches 100% of employee 401(k) contributions up to 4% of salary, plus
50% of the next 2%. This means an employee contributing 6% of salary receives a
5% match. Company matching contributions vest immediately — there is no vesting
schedule.

## Learning & Development

Every employee receives an annual learning and development budget of $2,000 that
can be spent on courses, books, conferences, and certifications. The budget does
not roll over year to year. Conference travel is covered separately under the
travel policy and does not count against the L&D budget.

## Wellness

Northwind provides a monthly wellness stipend of $50 that can be used for gym
memberships, fitness classes, or mental health apps. Employees also receive
access to a confidential Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offering up to 8 free
counseling sessions per year.

## Equity

Most full-time employees are eligible for stock options. Options vest over a
standard 4-year schedule with a 1-year cliff, meaning 25% vests after the first
year and the remainder vests monthly over the following three years.

## Other Perks

Employees receive a free lunch on anchor days (Tuesday and Thursday), a
$25 monthly commuter benefit, and a company phone plan discount.
