# Northwind Robotics — Atlas-7 Product Overview

## What Is Atlas-7?

Atlas-7 is Northwind Robotics' flagship autonomous warehouse robot, released in
March 2025. It is designed to transport shelving units and pallets within
fulfillment centers, working alongside human staff.

## Key Specifications

- Maximum payload: 1,200 kilograms.
- Top speed: 2.5 meters per second when unloaded and 1.6 meters per second when
  carrying maximum payload.
- Battery: a swappable lithium-iron-phosphate (LiFePO4) pack providing up to 11
  hours of continuous operation on a single charge.
- Charging: a depleted pack reaches 80% charge in 45 minutes using the Northwind
  fast-charging dock.
- Navigation: a combination of LiDAR, stereo cameras, and floor-mounted fiducial
  markers.

## Safety

Atlas-7 is rated to operate within 20 centimeters of a human before triggering a
controlled stop. It carries an emergency stop button on all four sides. The robot
complies with the ANSI/RIA R15.08 standard for industrial mobile robots.

## Fleet Management

Up to 200 Atlas-7 units can be coordinated by a single instance of the Northwind
Fleet Controller software. The Fleet Controller schedules tasks, balances battery
swaps, and routes robots to avoid congestion.

## Warranty and Support

Atlas-7 ships with a standard 2-year hardware warranty. An extended warranty
extends coverage to 5 years. Northwind offers a 99.5% parts-availability
guarantee for all supported units, and end-of-life support is guaranteed for at
least 7 years after a unit is purchased.

## Pricing

The base Atlas-7 unit is priced at $85,000. The Fleet Controller software is
licensed separately at $12,000 per year for up to 50 units.
