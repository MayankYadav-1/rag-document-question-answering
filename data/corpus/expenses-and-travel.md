# Northwind Robotics — Expense & Travel Policy

## General Reimbursement Rules

Employees may be reimbursed for reasonable business expenses. All expenses must
be submitted in Expensify within 30 days of the transaction date. Expenses
submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed except in documented emergencies.

Any single expense over $75 requires an itemized receipt. Expenses under $75 may
be submitted without a receipt but still require a business justification.

## Approval Limits

- Expenses up to $500 can be approved by your direct manager.
- Expenses between $500 and $5,000 require director approval.
- Expenses above $5,000 require VP approval and must be submitted at least two
  weeks in advance whenever possible.

## Travel Booking

All flights and hotels must be booked through the company travel portal, TravelHub.
Economy class is the standard for all domestic flights and any international
flight under 6 hours. Business class may be booked for international flights
longer than 6 hours with director approval.

Hotel spending should not exceed $250 per night in standard cities and $350 per
night in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, and Tokyo).

## Meals and Per Diem

When traveling, employees receive a meal per diem of $70 per day in the United
States and $90 per day internationally. The per diem does not require receipts.
Alcohol is not reimbursable under the per diem or otherwise, except for
pre-approved client entertainment.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle use for business is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile, consistent
with the current IRS standard mileage rate.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are never reimbursable: personal entertainment, traffic fines,
in-flight upgrades paid out of pocket, and hotel minibar charges.
