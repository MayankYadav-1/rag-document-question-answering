# Northwind Robotics — Paid Time Off & Leave Policy

## Vacation (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue paid time off at a rate of 1.5 days per month, for a
total of 18 days per year. PTO begins accruing on your first day of employment,
but it cannot be used during the first 90 days (the probationary period).

Unused PTO rolls over into the next calendar year, but the maximum balance you
may carry over is capped at 10 days. Any balance above the cap on December 31 is
forfeited. Northwind does not pay out unused PTO except where required by state
law at termination.

To request PTO, submit a request in Workday at least 5 business days in advance.
Requests longer than 10 consecutive days require director approval.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year,
granted in full on January 1 (they do not accrue monthly). Sick days do not roll
over and are not paid out. A doctor's note is required for any sick absence
longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave

Northwind offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all new parents,
regardless of gender, following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a
child. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event.
This benefit is available to employees who have completed at least 6 months of
service.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for an immediate family
member and up to 2 days for an extended family member.

## Jury Duty

Northwind provides paid leave for the full duration of jury service. There is no
cap on the number of days.
