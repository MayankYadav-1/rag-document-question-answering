# Northwind Robotics — Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

## Work Arrangements

Northwind operates on a hybrid model. Most employees are expected to be in the
office at least 3 days per week, with Tuesday and Thursday designated as
company-wide anchor days when in-person attendance is mandatory for office-based
staff.

Fully remote arrangements are available for specific roles and must be approved
by both your manager and the People team. Fully remote employees are expected to
travel to the nearest office once per quarter for team on-sites; travel costs for
these on-sites are covered by the company.

## Core Hours

Regardless of location, all employees must be available during core hours of
10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local time zone. Outside of core hours, employees
may set their own schedule as long as work is completed.

## Home Office Stipend

Remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time home office setup stipend of $1,000
to purchase a desk, chair, and other equipment. In addition, there is a recurring
$60 per month internet and utilities reimbursement for fully remote employees.

Equipment purchased with the stipend remains the property of Northwind Robotics
and must be returned upon termination if its value exceeds $250.

## Time Zones and Meetings

Teams that span more than three time zones should record key meetings and post
notes in the team channel within 24 hours so that no one is required to attend
meetings outside their core hours.

## Requesting a Change

To change your work arrangement, submit a request through the People portal. The
People team aims to respond to arrangement change requests within 10 business
days.
